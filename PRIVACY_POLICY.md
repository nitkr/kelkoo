# Privacy Policy for Kelkoo

Kelkoo is a personal sideload music app. It does not operate a commercial service or collect account data for the Kelkoo project maintainers.

Playback uses YouTube / YouTube Music network APIs (via the app’s client stack). Library, likes, playlists, and listen history may be stored **on your device**. Optional third-party integrations (if you enable them) are governed by those services.

We do not sell this app. For copyright or code concerns about this repository, open a GitHub issue on https://github.com/nitkr/kelkoo.
